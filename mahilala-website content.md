# Mahilala Madagascar Website Development Content Map for Claude/Cursor

## Purpose of this Markdown File

Use this file as the working implementation brief for building and iterating the Mahilala Madagascar website page by page. This is not a marketing summary. It is a development-facing content placement guide for Claude, Cursor, or any AI-assisted developer working inside the codebase.

The website is for **Mahilala Madagascar**, located in **Toliara, Madagascar**, on the domain **mahilalamadagascar.org**. The organization empowers youth through mentorship, civic engagement, environmental education, and leadership development.

Every page must communicate Mahilala Madagascar as a credible, warm, youth-centered, community-based organization. The site must not feel like a university page, a scientific institute page, or a generic NGO template.

## Highest-Priority Content Corrections from Client Comments

These updates come from the client review comments and should override placeholder wording in the original blueprint.

### 1. Meaning of “Mahilala”

Do not keep the original placeholder that says the name may relate to knowing, awakening, or enlightenment. Replace it with the client-confirmed meaning:

**Mahilala comes from a word meaning “Intelligence” in a language spoken in Southern Madagascar.**

For this website, intelligence must be explained as more than school results, memorization, academic diplomas, or classroom performance. Mahilala presents intelligence as a deeper life capacity that grows through experience, learning, personal development, self-improvement, open-mindedness, access to education, positive values, human connection, civic responsibility, and the desire to build a better future for society, the environment, and the economy.

The About page must present this as a central brand story moment. Use warm, reflective language. Avoid making it sound like a dictionary definition.

Suggested placement:

- Page: `/about`
- Section: “The Name Behind the Mission”
- Component: Full-width pale background section, centered text, large pull-quote typography
- Recommended section headline: **The Intelligence Behind Mahilala** or **The Name Behind the Mission**
- Recommended pull quote: **“At Mahilala, intelligence is a lifelong journey of learning, awareness, responsibility, and positive impact.”**

### 2. Story Behind Mahilala

The About page must explain that Mahilala was born from personal reflection and lived experience. The founder realized that many lessons and opportunities discovered over the past ten years could have changed the course of life if they had been known earlier. That realization became a desire to support young people facing different types of challenges, including financial difficulty, social struggles, family-related struggles, lack of guidance, and absence of strong role models.

The story must also explain that environmental protection has always been a core value of the organization because of the founder’s marine sciences and environmental background.

Important points to include:

- Mahilala supports youth not only financially, but through guidance, inspiration, personal development, values, leadership, and confidence.
- Many young people lack access to strong guidance or inspiring role models.
- Some adults who are expected to guide youth are also dealing with difficult realities themselves.
- Mahilala exists to become a source of inspiration, guidance, and empowerment.
- Mahilala believes the education system does not always allow every young person to fully develop their potential.
- Success should not depend only on memorization or academic performance.
- Youth also need human, social, emotional, creative, civic, and environmental intelligence.
- The initiative started informally in **2019** and was officially recognized by the Malagasy government in **2025**.

Suggested placement:

- Page: `/about`
- Section: “Our Story” or “The Story Behind Mahilala”
- Component: Two-column layout, text on the left, authentic image on the right
- Image suggestion: founder at a youth event, early Mahilala activity, community gathering, or coastal/environmental action

### 3. Youth Orientation and Toroy Izy Timeline

The youth orientation initiative began informally in **2016**. The page or online presence started being created in **2021**. The initiative became more public and meaningful when the founder shared a deeply personal story during a TEDx talk in Toliara in **December 2023**.

The TEDx message to preserve:

A person from a poor family background can still become an important contributor to society when they discover their purpose, believe in their potential, and choose to change the story of their family and future generations.

Use this content in:

### 4. Sekoly Manga Timeline and Programme Story

The Sekoly Manga initiative began in **2019** with the creation of the “Sekoly Manga Madagascar” page, which shared accessible environmental content. It later became part of Mahilala’s environmental education activities.

Official launch/activity context:

- Sekoly Manga was officially launched through collaboration with **BlueDays at School**, an initiative led by students from the **Institut Halieutique et des Sciences Marines (IH.SM)**.
- Students received practical field training to conduct environmental education activities in schools across Toliara.
- Mahilala acted as mentor and guide to the students.
- Many of those students are now contributing to society and environmental protection through their own initiatives and community engagement.
- In **October 2025**, Mahilala collaborated with **VOIZO Madagascar** to provide marine bioecology training for fishing communities along the southwest coast of Madagascar.
- The programme continues through collaborations and awareness activities, including World Environment Day 2026 initiatives alongside **DREDD** and **DREN**.

## Global Design Rules for Claude/Cursor

These rules must be implemented consistently across all pages and components.

### Typography

Use **DM Sans only** across the entire website.

Recommended scale:

- Hero title: DM Sans 700, 64px to 80px desktop, 36px to 48px mobile
- H1 section heading: DM Sans 700, 48px desktop, 32px mobile
- H2 subheading: DM Sans 600, 32px desktop, 24px mobile
- H3 card heading: DM Sans 600, 22px
- Overline/label: DM Sans 500, 12px, uppercase, 2px letter spacing
- Body text: DM Sans 400, 17px, line-height 1.7
- Caption: DM Sans 400, 14px
- Button text: DM Sans 600, 15px, uppercase, 1.5px letter spacing

### Color Tokens

Create a central constants file or Tailwind theme tokens using:

```ts
const colors = {
  deepOcean: '#0B2B40',
  warmSand: '#C8A96E',
  coastalGreen: '#2D6A4F',
  offWhite: '#F7F5F2',
  pureWhite: '#FFFFFF',
  darkText: '#1A1A1A',
  midText: '#555555',
  overlayBlack: 'rgba(0,0,0,0.55)',
}
```

### Visual Restrictions

Do not use:

- Blue-to-purple gradients
- Multi-color gradients
- Emoji icons
- Pill buttons
- Heavy shadows
- Colored shadows
- Over-rounded cards
- Stock-photo aesthetics
- Abstract SaaS-style illustrations

Use:

- Authentic photography
- Youth gatherings
- Coastal Madagascar scenery
- Founder speaking or mentoring
- Community events
- Environmental fieldwork
- Clean alternating backgrounds of white and off-white

### Cards

- Border radius: maximum 7px
- Suggested radius: 6px
- Shadow: `0 2px 12px rgba(0,0,0,0.07)` maximum
- Background: white or off-white only

### Buttons

Primary:

- Background: `#0B2B40`
- Text: white
- Radius: 5px
- Padding: 14px 32px

Secondary/outline:

- Border: 1.5px solid `#0B2B40`
- Background: transparent
- Text: `#0B2B40`

Accent CTA:

- Background: `#C8A96E`
- Text: white
- Radius: 5px

### Icons

Use only `react-icons`.

Recommended:

- `react-icons/fi`
- `react-icons/hi`
- `react-icons/bs`
- `react-icons/fa` for social icons

Do not use emojis in headings, cards, buttons, lists, or labels.

### Animation

Use GSAP and ScrollTrigger.

Standard entrance animation:

```ts
fromTo(element, { opacity: 0, y: 40 }, { opacity: 1, y: 0, duration: 0.8, ease: 'power2.out' })
```

Use stagger of `0.12` for grids.

Animate:

- Section headings
- Card grids
- Image reveals
- Stat counters
- Programme blocks
- Timeline nodes

Do not animate every tiny element. Use animation to guide attention, not distract.

### Sliders

Use Swiper.js for:

- Homepage hero
- Testimonials
- Programme highlights if needed
- Gallery sliders

Hero slider:

- 3 to 5 slides
- Autoplay 5000ms

# Accessibility and Performance Checklist

Claude/Cursor must enforce these before considering the site complete.

## Accessibility

- WCAG 2.1 AA minimum
- Keyboard navigation for all interactive elements
- Visible focus states using `outline: 2px solid #C8A96E`
- Sliders must support keyboard arrows
- Icon-only buttons must have `aria-label`
- Images must have descriptive alt text
- Do not use empty alt text unless image is purely decorative
- Text on dark backgrounds must pass contrast requirements
- Color alone must not convey meaning

## Performance

- Use `next/image`
- Use WebP images where possible
- Compress above-fold images, target below 200KB where realistic
- Lazy-load below-fold images
- Import only required Swiper modules
- GSAP markers disabled in production
- Google Fonts preconnect in head
- Core Web Vitals targets:
  - LCP below 2.5s
  - CLS below 0.1
  - INP/FID below 100ms target where possible
